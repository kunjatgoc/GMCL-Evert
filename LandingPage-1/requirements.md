## Prompt

Build a single-page, responsive landing page for a trading competition called "Global Market Champion League," hosted by a forex/trading broker called NewEra Broker. Keep the page simple and conversion-focused  do not add extra sections like testimonials, leaderboards, FAQs, blog, or a large footer. Only build the 5 sections listed below, in this exact order.

Design style: modern, premium, finance/trading industry aesthetic with a dark black + green glossy theme.
- Background: deep black / near-black (#0A0A0A – #121212) throughout the page.
- Accent color: vibrant shades of green (like #00FF87, #00C853, or neon/emerald green) used for headings, buttons, icons, borders, and highlights.
- Glossy/premium feel: use subtle gradients (black to dark green), soft glowing effects around buttons and cards (green glow/shadow on hover), glassmorphism cards with slight transparency and blur, and thin glowing green borders on key elements.
- Buttons should have a glossy green gradient fill with a soft glow/shadow, smooth hover animation (slight scale-up or brightness increase).
- Typography: clean, bold, modern sans-serif for headings (white or bright green), lighter gray/white for body text for readability against the dark background.
- Add subtle green particle, glow, or gradient blob effects in the background of the hero section for a premium, high-energy trading vibe.
- Generous spacing, smooth hover effects, and fully mobile-responsive layout.

**1. Hero Section**
- Headline: "Global Market Champion League"
- Subtext: Ask the visitor if they're good at trading, invite them to join and win prizes. Mention that new traders (freshers) can join and get a $10,000 USD demo account to experience live trading risk-free.
- Primary CTA button: "Join the League"  should smoothly scroll down to the registration form section.
- Include a relevant background visual or graphic element related to trading/finance/growth charts (no real stock photos of people needed).

**2. Trust/Branding Bar**
- A slim, minimal horizontal section right below the hero.
- Text: "Powered by NewEra Broker"
- An input field or short instructional line asking the user to enter their Demo ID.

**3. About the League Section**
- Heading: "About the League"
- Content: The league runs from 7th September to 18th September. Every participant starts with a $10,000 USD demo account. The winner is the trader who grows their account balance the most by the end of the league.
- Use icons or small visual accents for the date, starting capital, and win condition to break up the text.

**4. Prizes Section**
- Heading: "Prizes"
- Display 3 prize cards side-by-side (stack vertically on mobile):
  - 1st Place: $100,000
  - 2nd Place: $50,000
  - 3rd Place: $25,000
- Use a podium-style visual hierarchy (1st place card slightly larger or highlighted) with a trophy/medal icon per card.

**5. Registration Form Section**
- Heading: "Join the League Now"
- Form fields: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number (with country code selector/dropdown)
- Submit button: "Register Now"
- Add basic client-side validation (required fields, valid email format, valid phone number format).

Technical requirements:
- Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- Smooth scroll navigation from hero CTA to the form
- Clean component structure (Hero, TrustBar, AboutLeague, Prizes, RegistrationForm)
- No unnecessary sections, no filler content beyond what's specified above
